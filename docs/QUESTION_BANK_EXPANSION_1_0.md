# IT Coach Question Bank Expansion 1.0

The prototype question bank has been expanded with additional author-generated practice items and a difficulty field.

## Difficulty
- foundation: initial/core check
- developing: additional evidence for practice and reassessment

## Reassessment behavior
The reassessment engine attempts to exclude prompts used in the prior attempt and prefers alternate difficulty evidence when available.

## Production requirement
These additional questions are prototype material, not official CXC questions or marking guidance. Before production release, every item should be reviewed against the final approved workbook and teacher materials, tagged to the exact objective/skill, and expanded into sufficient alternate forms to support reliable reassessment.
